## 1. Publication-Ready Post Copy

**Post Hook:** Founders: Before handing $10k to paid acquisition channels, verify these 5 engineering checkpoints.

**Post Body:**
Paid performance marketing magnifies product funnel dynamics. If your retention curves are leaking, ad spend simply accelerates cash burn.

Before scaling customer acquisition budget, high-scale engineering teams verify these 5 foundational checkpoints:

1. Server-Side Telemetry Fidelity (<1% Drop):
Verify that core activation and revenue milestones are dispatched server-side. Pure client-side tracking loses 15–25% of conversions to ad blockers.

2. Measured Time-to-First-Value (<15 Minutes):
Track the exact timestamp delta between signup commit and first meaningful utility. If users require 45 minutes of configuration, 60% churn.

3. Flattening Cohort Retention Curves:
Examine your Day-30 retention trajectory. If the curve continuously decays toward zero, you do not yet have product-market fit. Never scale spend into a leaky bucket.

4. Strict UTM Taxonomy Governance:
Enforce standardized lowercase campaign parameters with mandatory `utm_content` tags. Linking social spend to CRM pipeline requires clean attribution links.

5. Automated Friction Alerting:
Configure alerts on onboarding milestone conversion rates. If a release degrades step-2 completion by 10%, engineering should know before finance does.
