AUDIT 1 & 2: SCHEMAS & ORPHANS

Edge Validation & Orphan Events

Two structural audits to eliminate corrupt or unnecessary data before it enters your warehouse:

Audit 1: Schema Enforcement at the Edge

Problem: Flexible JSON schemas sound convenient until frontend sends strings where integers are expected.
Fix: Enforce strict Pydantic validation at the gateway. Quarantine malformed payloads before they reach downstream models.

Audit 2: Orphan Event Pruning

Problem: Deprecated features from 2024 still fire 50,000 events a day, inflating cloud analytics storage costs.
Fix: Query events with zero dashboard queries over 90 days. Safely sunset unused telemetry hooks.

AUDIT 3 & 4: TIMESTAMPS & DEDUPE

UTC Normalization & Idempotency

Ensure cohort metrics and retention curves reflect true user activity:

Audit 3: Mandatory Server-Side UTC

Problem: Client device clocks are frequently inaccurate or configured in local time zones, distorting Day-1 retention cohorts.
Fix: Assign ISO-8601 UTC timestamps at the server ingestion boundary. Never rely on device clocks for analytical ordering.

Audit 4: Idempotent Event Deduplication

Problem: Mobile network reconnects resend HTTP requests, double-counting signup conversions.
Fix: Generate deterministic SHA-256 fingerprints and enforce unique constraints in the analytics database.

AUDIT 5 & 6: UTMS & SECURITY

UTM Taxonomies & Injection Defense

Protect your attribution reporting and spreadsheet consumers:

Audit 5: Programmatic Lowercase UTMs

Problem: Inconsistent casing like utm_source=LinkedIn vs linkedin splits marketing attribution into disjoint buckets.
Fix: Enforce strict lowercase transforms and validated platform enums in all link builders.

Audit 6: CSV Formula Injection Protection

Problem: Telemetry values starting with =, +, -, or @ can execute arbitrary formulas in spreadsheet applications.
Fix: Sanitize exports by prefixing potentially dangerous leading characters with a single quote.

Automated Telemetry Health Monitoring

Configure automated alerts to catch pipeline anomalies before stakeholders notice:

CRITICAL ALERT THRESHOLDS
