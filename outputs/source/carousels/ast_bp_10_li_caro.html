THE CONTENT BOTTLENECK

The Manual Repurposing Trap

Why traditional social content production breaks down at technical SaaS companies:

1. Manual Rewrite Fatigue

Engineers spend hours explaining features to marketers, who spend days rewording technical documentation into generic social updates.

2. Context Dilution

Simplifying complex architectural concepts often removes the exact nuances that make technical buyers trust your product.

3. Format Laziness

Copy-pasting identical text across LinkedIn, X, and YouTube ignores native platform algorithms and engagement mechanics.

Deterministic Source Ingestion

2. Quantitative Metric Identification

STEP 3: QUALITY GATES

The Pre-Publish Verification Gate

Never publish without verifying all 8 readiness criteria:

1. Hook Tested: Under 150 chars, clear value proposition.
2. Evidence Checked: All numeric claims backed by source text.
3. Rights Verified: Fonts and graphics licensed for commercial use.
4. Active Tracking URL: Programmatic UTM parameters verified.
5. Rendered File Present: MP4, PDF, or PNG exists on disk.
6. Editable Source Preserved: Markdown or SVG available for revision.
