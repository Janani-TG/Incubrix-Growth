**Headline / Hook:**  
The 30-Day Growth Engineering Sprint: 3 Core Principles for Modern B2B SaaS Telemetry.

**Post Body:**  
Over the past month, we ran an exhaustive operational growth sprint across 30 technical assets—spanning architecture carousels, telemetry video breakdowns, and cohort teardowns.

When you treat growth as an engineering discipline rather than an ad-hoc promotional broadcast, the data tells a remarkably clear story.

Here are the 3 core principles every technical founder should implement:

1. **Evidence Consistently Beats Hype:**  
   Software engineers and technical buyers don't engage with generic marketing platitudes or unverified performance claims. Content that walks through real SQL queries, data flow diagrams, and failure retrospectives drove our highest engagement depth and qualification rates.

2. **Disciplined Repurposing Saves 70% of Production Overhead:**  
   A single rigorous architectural blueprint can anchor a technical long-form post, a 6-slide visual carousel, and a 45-second vertical breakdown. Building reusable content primitives accelerates velocity without sacrificing technical depth.

3. **Attribution Hygiene Protects Capital:**  
   If your UTM taxonomy is fragmented or your event schemas lack server-side validation, your ROI calculations are fictional. Implementing strict naming conventions and idempotent webhook ingestion provides clarity on true pipeline attribution.

Growth isn't magic. It's instrumentation, rapid hypothesis testing, and continuous optimization.
