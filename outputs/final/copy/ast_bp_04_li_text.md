## 1. Publication-Ready Long-Form Post Copy

**Headline / Hook:**  
If your weekly growth dashboard only shows total pageviews and raw signups, you are flying blind.

**Post Body:**  
Pageviews don't pay server bills, and unactivated signups don't create enterprise value.

When software teams scale ad spend based on surface-level vanity metrics, they often mask severe churn dynamics happening beneath the surface.

Here are the 4 core telemetry signals technical founders should demand on their primary Monday morning dashboard:

1. **Time-to-First-Value (TTV):**  
   The exact elapsed time from account creation to first core milestone execution. If TTV exceeds 15 minutes, Day-7 retention decays rapidly.

2. **Cohort Day-7 Stabilization Rate:**  
   Does your cohort curve flatten into a healthy plateau, or does it decay continuously to zero? A flattening curve signals true product-market fit.

3. **Multi-User Collaboration Depth:**  
   Single-player accounts churn at triple the rate of multi-seat teams. Measure the ratio of signups who invite a teammate within 48 hours.

4. **Attributed Pipeline Velocity:**  
   Which specific content pillar and campaign drove qualified pipeline, not just clicks? Track leads through to closed-won revenue using clean, server-side UTM parameters.

Stop celebrating top-of-funnel traffic spikes until you know your retention loop is sealed.
